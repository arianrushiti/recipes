Beef and Broccoli

Prep15 min
Cook15 min
Total30 min
Servings4
DifficultyEasy
CuisineAsian-inspired

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Slice the steak into thin strips.

  2. 2

    Toss the steak with the light soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, white pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and baking soda. Set aside while you prep the broccoli.

  3. 3

    Cut the broccoli into pieces. Boil in a HexClad pot of water for about 2 minutes, then move to ice water.

  4. 4

    Whisk the sauce: beef broth, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, ginger paste, sugar, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and MSG.

  5. 5

    Sear the steak in a HexClad pan on medium-high heat until browned on each side, then remove it.

  6. 6

    Add the garlic to the same pan and cook until fragrant, then deglaze with a splash of Shaoxing wine.

  7. 7

    Return the broccoli and steak to the pan, add the sauce, and bring to a simmer.

  8. 8

    Stir the cornstarch and water together, add to the pan, and cook until the sauce thickens. Serve over rice.
