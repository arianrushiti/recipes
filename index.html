Chipotle Steak Rice Bowl

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Total35 min
Servings2
DifficultyEasy
CuisineChipotle-inspired

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Marinate ribeye in chipotle peppers in adobo. Refrigerate 1-2 hours, or overnight.

  2. 2

    Sear in a hot oiled HexClad pan, 2 minutes per side. Season with salt and cumin while cooking. Rest, then slice.

  3. 3

    Cook washed rice with 2 cups water and a drizzle of oil. Boil, then cover on low until tender.

  4. 4

    Stir oil, cilantro, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and lime juice into the rice.

  5. 5

    Serve steak over the rice.

Chipotle Chicken Rice Bowl

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Total35 min
Servings2
DifficultyEasy
CuisineChipotle-inspired

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Coat chicken thighs in minced chipotle peppers in adobo. Marinate 30 minutes, or up to overnight.

  2. 2

    Cook washed rice with 2 cups water and a drizzle of oil. Boil, then cover on low until tender.

  3. 3

    Sear chicken in a hot oiled HexClad pan, about 4 minutes per side, until charred and cooked through. Rest, then dice.

  4. 4

    Stir oil, cilantro, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and lime juice into the rice.

  5. 5

    Serve diced chicken over the rice.

Viral Costco Chicken Rice

Prep10 min
Cook20 min
Total30 min
Servings4
DifficultyEasy
CuisineAsian-inspired

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Rinse 2 cups white rice and cook it in a HexClad pot with 2 cups water. Bring to a boil, cover, and cook on low for 15 minutes, then rest 5 minutes.

  2. 2

    Shred the rotisserie chicken and discard the skin and bones.

  3. 3

    Stir the chili paste, soy sauce, and sesame oil into the hot rice.

  4. 4

    Fold in the shredded chicken and green onions and serve.

High-Protein Baked Mac and Cheese

Prep20 min
Cook30 min
Total50 min
Servings12
DifficultyMedium
CuisineAmerican

Ingredients

Steps

  1. 1

    Preheat oven to 350F and lightly grease a 9x13 inch baking dish.

  2. 2

    Grate all three cheeses, toss together, and divide in half. One half goes in the sauce, the other is for layering and topping.

  3. 3

    Blend the cottage cheese and Fairlife milk on high for 30 to 45 seconds until completely smooth.

  4. 4

    Boil the cavatappi in salted water for 1 to 2 minutes less than the package directions for al dente. Drain and set aside.

  5. 5

    Melt the butter in a large HexClad pot over medium heat. Whisk in the flour, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper for about 1 minute until bubbly and fragrant.

  6. 6

    Slowly whisk in the evaporated milk until smooth, then whisk in the blended cottage cheese and milk plus the Dijon mustard. Simmer gently for 3 to 4 minutes until it coats the back of a spoon.

  7. 7

    Turn heat to low. Add half the cheese blend a handful at a time, stirring until melted before adding more.

  8. 8

    Fold the pasta into the sauce. Spread half in the dish, top with half the remaining cheese, layer the rest of the pasta, and finish with the last of the cheese.

  9. 9

    Bake uncovered for 25 minutes until bubbling at the edges. Broil 1 to 2 minutes, watching closely, until golden brown. Rest 5 to 10 minutes before serving.
